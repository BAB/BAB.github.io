Summary

Technology leader with more than twenty years of experience in enterprise applications, technology governance, and responsible AI adoption, with a lasting focus on helping people become capable and confident with technology.

For more than twenty years I've served the National Spiritual Assembly of the Bahá'ís of the United States through its IT department, helping build the platforms its work depends on. Today I lead the department's strategic initiatives: governance of our organizational AI work, the design of a volunteer management process and its supporting systems, and extending national IT capacity to thirteen regional councils.

Much of my work has been in enterprise applications. I directed the development of our Dynamics 365 CRMs, four systems on the Power Platform used by the national community down to individual members, and I serve as IT Liaison on our Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations ERP implementation. Before this role I led our IT PMO, and I sit on our IT Leadership team and Architecture Review Team.

The thread through all of it is helping people become capable and confident with technology. That started at Perry Technical Institute, where I founded the Digital Imaging Workshop and went on to teach, and it carried through natural foods retail systems at Good Earth Natural Foods and Whole Foods Market, web development at Sapient, and co-founding Digital Campaigns in San Francisco. These days it looks like a leveled AI learning pathway, careful guidance on responsible AI use, and clear paths for good ideas to grow into lasting systems.
